PuchiPay Child Safety Standards

PuchiPay is committed to maintaining a safe platform and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Prohibited Content and Conduct

PuchiPay strictly prohibits the creation, upload, sharing, promotion, solicitation, distribution, or facilitation of content or behavior involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

This includes, but is not limited to, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking of children for sexual purposes, solicitation of sexual content involving minors, and other conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child.

Reporting Safety Concerns

Users may report accounts, posts, messages, voice-room activity, or other content that violates PuchiPay's safety standards through the reporting features available within the PuchiPay application.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as high-priority safety matters.

Enforcement

When PuchiPay becomes aware of content or activity involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we may remove the content, restrict or permanently disable accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required, and take other appropriate safety measures.

Reporting to Authorities

PuchiPay complies with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Where required by applicable law, confirmed instances of child sexual abuse material or exploitation may be reported to appropriate regional, national, or international authorities or designated child-safety organizations.

Cooperation with Authorities

PuchiPay may cooperate with lawful requests from law-enforcement agencies and child-safety authorities in accordance with applicable law and our privacy obligations.

Child Safety Contact

Questions, notifications, or reports concerning child safety and PuchiPay's CSAE prevention practices may be directed to the official PuchiPay child safety contact.
